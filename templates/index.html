Popular Questions

What permits do I need for a new factory?

A new factory typically requires a NEMA permit for environmental compliance (e.g., EIA), a DOSH license for occupational health and safety, and local council permits. We assist with all these submissions.

How often should fire extinguishers be serviced?

In line with regional standards, fire extinguishers require annual maintenance (servicing and testing) and typically a hydrostatic test every five years.

Do you offer customized safety training?

Yes, we specialize in site-specific safety training, tailoring the content and practical demonstrations to your unique industrial environment and equipment.
